Embedding the simulator

Each demo below is an <iframe> of the simulator with ?embed in its link. It shows play/pause, reset and the speed menu, starts playing the first time it is scrolled into view, and after that its play button is the reader's.

Gathering

20 robots, async scheduler. Each one moves to the average of all robots; they meet at one point.

Smallest Enclosing Circle

12 robots. Each one moves to the nearest point of the smallest circle around them all; they end on that circle (drawn).

Uniform Circle Formation

10 robots spread on a circle at even spacing.

Go-To-Center on a chain of three robots

Limited sight (100): each robot sees only its neighbour. One robot at a time, a turn per second. This one also has the step buttons, and the dark theme.

SqGathering, a turn at a time

6 robots, one at a time (sequential scheduler), a turn per second, with a caption for each turn.
